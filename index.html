Military12–13 Sep

Russia launches 453 strike drones overnight; Ukrainian air defences neutralise 409; hits at 13 locations including Odesa and near Polish border; Ukrainian forces strike two Russian refineries

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported that from 18:00 on 12 September Russia attacked with 453 strike UAVs (Shahed including jet-powered, Gerbera, Parodiya decoys) and S8000 Banderol missile drones launched from Oryol, Kursk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, occupied Crimea and the Sea of Azov. As of 08:00 on 13 September air defences had shot down or suppressed 405 drones and four Banderol; hits at 13 locations and debris at seven. Regional administrations reported civilian casualties and damage in Odesa, Donetsk, Kherson, Mykolaiv, Chernihiv, Dnipropetrovsk, Zaporizhzhia and Volyn (train locomotive and gas station near the Polish border). Ukrainian General Staff and Defence Ministry stated overnight strikes on the Slavyansk EKO refinery (Krasnodar region) and the TANECO refinery (Nizhnekamsk, Tatarstan), with fires reported. Zelenskyy noted roughly 2,100 attack drones, 1,700 guided bombs and 78 missiles over the preceding week. Independent wires (Reuters, AFP, AP, RFE/RL) corroborated the Ukrainian Air Force numbers, locations and approximate civilian impact ranges from official Ukrainian sources.
