Military7–8 Oct

The 6–7 October package still stands; no new overnight Air Force tally was opened before cutoff

Reporting

Interfax-Ukraine, restating the Air Force, said that as of 09:00 on 7 October defenders had shot down or suppressed 161 targets: five Iskander-M/KN-23/S-400/Zircon/Onyx ballistic missiles, 39 Kh-101, Iskander-K and Kalibr cruise missiles, with six further cruise missiles still being clarified, and 117 drones of which 38 were jet-powered. The same note said the attack ran from 18:00 on 6 October and used Zircon/Onyx from Rostov, ballistic missiles from Bryansk and Voronezh, 48 cruise missiles from Vologda, Kursk and the Caspian, and 130 Shahed-type drones (46 jet-powered) plus Gerbera and Parodiya decoys, with hits at 18 locations and debris at five. Kyiv Post carried the same Air Force figures. Reuters reported President Zelenskyy saying the strikes killed at least 25 people nationwide, including 19 in Pryluky of whom five were children, with 100 injured, and that Russia had fired 70 missiles.
