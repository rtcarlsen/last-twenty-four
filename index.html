Military29 Sep

Russian combined strikes continued through 29 September: ballistic missiles and jet-powered drones against Kyiv; glide bombs on Sumy during school hours; damage and civilian casualties reported in Odesa and other oblasts; Zelenskyy said more than 100 drones launched since morning of 29 September including 29 jet-powered; long-range Ukrainian operations for October approved in a Kyiv staff meeting

Reporting

Associated Press, drawing on the Ukrainian Air Force and local administrations, reported that Russia fired ballistic missiles and dozens of jet-powered drones at Kyiv on 29 September; Ukrainian air defences claimed two ballistic-missile intercepts. Damage was reported to apartment buildings, houses and public transport in the capital. In Sumy, President Zelenskyy said teachers and students evacuated a school shortly before four glide bombs struck during classes; two people were killed in a nearby residential area. Odesa regional officials reported eight wounded overnight and damage to residential buildings, a school, an animal shelter and port infrastructure. Russia’s Defence Ministry claimed strikes on data-processing centres in Kyiv said to support Ukrainian military AI use, plus drone and cruise-missile storage, an electrical substation, a drone plant and Odesa port facilities. In a first-person post at 18:52 UTC on 29 September, Zelenskyy said more than 100 drones had been launched since that morning; as of 18:00 local, 29 were jet-powered and 18 of those were shot down, with most conventional Shaheds also claimed downed. He thanked partners for a recently arrived NASAMS interceptor package and said Patriot-missile talks continue. Earlier the same afternoon he said a meeting with the Defence Ministry had approved Ukrainian long-range operations for October, identified F-16 sustainment packages with named partner countries, and selected first prototypes for a jet-Shahed counter system. The Ukrainian General Staff morning update covering 28 September recorded 213 combat engagements, 86 air strikes with 253 guided bombs, and claimed 1,440 Russian personnel losses. ISW published its 29 September campaign assessment in the early hours of 30 September CEST.
