Ukrainian deep-strike operation hits Novorossiysk naval base and Admiral Essen; Russian strikes continue across Ukraine

Reporting

Ukraine’s Defence Intelligence (HUR) and Unmanned Systems Forces reported overnight 8–9 September strikes on infrastructure at the Novorossiysk naval base and the Project 11356 frigate Admiral Essen (Kalibr carrier), causing a fire on the superstructure. Ukrainian sources also claimed destruction of an Su-33 and Mi-8 near Anapa and radar hits near Gelendzhik. Russian Krasnodar governor Kondratyev reported four killed (including a child) and 29 injured in Novorossiysk from the drone attack; mayor claimed debris on industrial sites. Reuters and independent monitors confirmed fires at the fuel-oil terminal. Concurrently, Russian forces continued missile and drone strikes across Ukraine, including Kyiv residential buildings; Ukrainian officials reported multiple civilian casualties (varying tallies of at least 12 killed and dozens injured across regions in the 24-hour window). Air Force reported 165 of 196 drones downed overnight into 9 September.
