Military16–17 Sep

Russia launches combined missile-and-drone package overnight; Ukrainian Air Force reports 157 drones plus ballistic, cruise and Oniks missiles; 131 aerial assets neutralised; hits at 36 locations; injuries in Kyiv, Odesa and Zaporizhzhia; Ukrainian forces strike Yaroslavl oil refinery and Rostov military airfield

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported that on the night of 16–17 September Russian forces launched approximately 157 Shahed-type (more than half jet-powered), Gerbera and Parodiya decoy drones from Oryol, Millerovo, occupied Donetsk and occupied Crimea, together with Oniks anti-ship missiles from Kursk, Iskander-M/S-400/KN-23 ballistic missiles from Bryansk and Voronezh, four Kalibr cruise missiles from the Caspian, and ten Banderol/Dan-T missiles. As of early morning 17 September air defences had destroyed or suppressed 131 aerial assets (124 drones and seven Banderol/Dan-T). Hits were recorded at 36 locations and debris at five. Kyiv mayor Klitschko reported 16–20 injured including two children; Odesa regional authorities reported seven injured including two children; Zaporizhzhia reported further casualties. Russian Defence Ministry claimed strikes on metallurgical, electronics, defence-industrial, energy and transport facilities in the same three oblasts and on vessels at Chornomorsk. Separately, President Zelenskyy stated that Ukrainian Security Service, special operations, military intelligence and foreign intelligence forces struck the Yaroslavl oil refinery and a military airfield in the Rostov region, damaging an An-12, two An-26s and three helicopters. Independent wires (Reuters, AFP, AP) and Ukrainian official channels corroborated the Ukrainian Air Force numbers and the reciprocal Ukrainian claims.

Open-source reporting showed no decisive change of control on the ground front.
