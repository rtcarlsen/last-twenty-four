Military18–19 Sep

Russian overnight package of 174 UAVs (Shahed/Gerbera/Parodiya, roughly half jet-powered) plus two Zircon anti-ship missiles; Ukrainian Air Force reports 141 aerial targets neutralised; hits at 15 locations; regional authorities report 8–11 killed and dozens injured across Kharkiv, Sumy, Odesa and other oblasts; Russian MoD claims Geran-4 strikes on two commercial vessels in the Black Sea

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force stated that overnight into 19 September Russian forces launched two Zircon anti-ship missiles from Kursk region and 174 strike and decoy UAVs (Shahed-type, Gerbera, Parodiya, Banderol/Dan-T) from Oryol, Millerovo, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, occupied Donetsk and Hvardiiske in occupied Crimea. As of 08:00 air defences had destroyed or suppressed 141 targets (138 drones and three Banderol/Dan-T). Hits were recorded at 15 locations and debris at nine; the two Zircons did not reach targets. Regional governors reported civilian casualties: five killed and 15 injured in Kharkiv oblast (including a double-tap in Balakliia), three killed and five injured in Kherson, further deaths and injuries in Sumy and Odesa; aggregate open-source tallies reached 8–11 killed and over 64 injured across the day. Separately, Russia’s Ministry of Defence claimed Geran-4 loitering munitions struck a Ukrainian dry-cargo ship en route to Odesa and a tanker in Odesa port, asserting both carried military supplies. Ukrainian General Staff reported strikes on Russian UAV control posts, a training range and support facilities in occupied Donetsk and Zaporizhzhia on 18–19 September. Independent wires (Reuters, AFP, Al Jazeera) and Ukrainian official channels corroborated the Air Force volumes and regional casualty reports; Russian claims on the vessels remain single-source from MoD/TASS.
