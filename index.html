Reporting

Ukrainian regional and national authorities reported that Russian forces conducted sustained drone and guided aerial-bomb attacks on 28 September, with confirmed civilian fatalities of at least six to eight across Kyiv region, Dnipro, Kharkiv and other locations, and dozens wounded (including children). Mayor Ihor Terekhov of Kharkiv reported a glide-bomb strike on a nine-storey residential building that injured more than 35–43 people, among them children. In Kyiv a daytime strike damaged a building of the National Academy of Sciences and caused at least one fatality; data centres, petrol stations and residential structures were also hit. President Zelenskyy characterised the pattern as “terrorising” civilians and noted the previous week’s aggregate of more than 2,200 attack drones, ~1,650 aerial bombs and dozens of missiles. Russia’s Defence Ministry claimed strikes on logistics hubs, data centres, naval-related sites and vessels linked to the Ukrainian military. Separately, Ukraine’s Third Army Corps stated it had recaptured an additional 51 km², bringing claimed total gains under the current phase to approximately 176 km²; Russian MoD claimed capture of two villages. Ukrainian sources and OSINT accounts reported fires at oil-depot and defence-plant sites in Krasnodar Krai and related facilities. Independent wires (Reuters, AFP, AP, dpa) distributed the principal Ukrainian casualty and damage claims and the Russian MoD target list; exact damage inventories at named Russian industrial sites remain single-source pending visual or industry corroboration.
