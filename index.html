Diplomatic4 Sep 2026, 19:13 CEST

Zelenskyy: Witkoff and Kushner to Moscow then Kyiv on Sunday

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy’s official X account said at 17:13 UTC on 4 September that “it has been clearly established that U.S. envoys will be in Ukraine and Russia in the coming days,” naming Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, and that they “will visit Moscow and then Kyiv on Sunday.”[1] He said Ukraine would “ensure the normal functioning of Russian airspace so that the negotiators can arrive safely” and would not conduct air strikes “for the time needed to hold these talks,” asking Russia to do the same. Earlier the same afternoon he said he had spoken with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte about the strikes, defense needs, and the U.S. visits, and that Rutte had discussed PURL financing and Patriot missiles.[2] Associated Press, carried by PBS NewsHour, reported the same itinerary and that this would be the envoys’ first visit to Kyiv; it cited Kremlin aide Yury Ushakov that a long-term settlement “can't be expected without solving the territorial issue.”[3] Stake: Zelenskyy’s X is a tier-1 Ukrainian presidential channel. Ushakov is a Russian presidential aide. AP is tier 2.

Judgment: a Sunday shuttle is likely to occur; a ceasefire text from it is unlikely. Confidence in the visit claim is high on Zelenskyy’s on-the-record itinerary and moderate on Russian reciprocity for the air pause.

Confidence: Moderate · Zelenskyy official X; AP/PBS
