MilitaryOvernight 11–12 Sep

Russia launches combined missile and drone strike; Odesa hardest hit; at least 11 killed, ~96 injured

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported that from the evening of 11 September Russia launched a combined attack including Iskander-M/KN-23 ballistic missiles, eight Kalibr cruise missiles, six Kh-59/69 air-launched missiles and approximately 129 attack drones (Shahed-type, jet-powered and decoys). Air defence shot down or suppressed 110 targets. Hits at about 20 locations. Odesa Oblast hardest hit with at least 35 injured including a five-month-old. Aggregate toll roughly 11 killed and 96 injured. Ukrainian forces claimed strikes near Taganrog and on Russian industrial sites. General Staff reported ~1,500 Russian personnel losses in 24 hours (cumulative ~1,505,380).

Equities check

Ukrainian Air Force, General Staff and oblast administrations are primary Ukrainian claim-carriers. Russian MoD/TASS are primary Russian claim-carriers. Independent wires and Kyiv Independent / Ukrinform corroborate locations and casualty ranges. ISW of 11 September noted no decisive ground advances.

Judgment

Russia almost certainly continues a high-tempo combined drone-and-missile campaign against Ukrainian urban and industrial targets. Ukrainian reciprocal long-range strikes remain a pressure tool. Ground lines showed no major change in open reporting.

Confidence

Moderate-to-high on strike volume and civilian impact; moderate on exact platform mixes and Russian personnel tallies.

Diplomatic12 Sep

Zelenskyy says he is ready to meet Putin at G20 summit in Miami if both attend

Reporting

In a Deutsche Welle interview released 12 September, President Zelenskyy said he would meet Putin on the sidelines of the G20 in Miami in December if both are invited, stressing results over rhetoric. Peskov/TASS said Russia had not decided on attendance and repeated the Moscow precondition. U.S. envoys Witkoff and Kushner had visited both capitals; no public breakthrough.

Equities check

Zelenskyy’s interview is primary. Peskov/TASS speak for the Kremlin. Wires corroborate public positions.

Judgment

Public signalling leaves open a possible leaders’ encounter under U.S. G20 presidency, but Russian preconditions make a substantive bilateral meeting far from certain.

Confidence

High on public statements; low on private content or any December meeting.
