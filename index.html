War and diplomacy

Military12 Sep

Russian combined strikes kill at least six to nine civilians; Odesa, Zaporizhzhia, Kryvyi Rih and Kramatorsk hit; 410 daytime UAVs reported

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported that on 12 September Russia launched 410 attack UAVs (37 jet-powered) between 06:30 and 18:00; air defences shot down or suppressed 336, with 21 hits recorded and roughly 55 still tracked at 18:00. Overnight into 12 September a combined package of ballistic, Kalibr, Kh-59/69 and drones struck multiple oblasts; Odesa region reported 35–37 injured including an infant. Governors and emergency services reported civilian deaths in Zaporizhzhia (at least two), Kryvyi Rih, Kramatorsk (three in vehicle strikes) and Zviahel/Zhytomyr (grocery store). Aggregate open tallies for the day ranged from six to nine killed and dozens injured. Russian MoD claimed hits on Zaporizhstal, Interpipe and Kryvyi Rih plants and a cargo ship at Odesa. Ukrainian General Staff reported 206 combat engagements, with the highest density near Pokrovsk, and continued long-range strikes on Russian chemical facilities in Samara/Tolyatti and Perm. Wires (Reuters, AP, AFP, RFE/RL) corroborated locations and casualty ranges from Ukrainian officials.

Equities check

Ukrainian Air Force, General Staff and oblast administrations are primary Ukrainian claim-carriers. Russian MoD and TASS are primary Russian claim-carriers. Independent wires provide second-tier corroboration of locations and approximate tolls but cannot independently verify platform mixes or industrial damage claims.

Judgment

Russia almost certainly sustained a high-tempo combined drone-and-missile campaign against Ukrainian urban, energy-adjacent and industrial targets on 12 September. Ukrainian reciprocal long-range strikes continue. Open-source ground reporting showed no decisive change of control near Pokrovsk or elsewhere in the window.

Confidence

Moderate-to-high on strike volume, locations and civilian impact; moderate on exact platform counts and Russian industrial-damage claims.
