Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported that from the evening of 24 September into 25 September Russian forces launched approximately 295 attack drones of various types, including 52 jet-powered Shahed-class systems, plus decoy platforms, from multiple launch directions. Air defences claimed 267 drones destroyed or suppressed. Debris and residual strikes were recorded at multiple locations, including residential districts in Kyiv (one 14-year-old killed, several injured) and a farm in Kharkiv Oblast (six agricultural workers killed in a Banderol-class strike on a vegetable storage facility on the preceding day, with the toll carried into the 25 September reporting cycle). General Staff morning bulletin recorded 236 combat engagements over the preceding day, with the highest density on the Pokrovsk axis. Independent wires (Reuters, AFP, AP) and Ukrainian official channels corroborated the scale of the drone package and the principal civilian fatality reports; Russian MoD claimed strikes on military-linked and logistics targets.
