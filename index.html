Moscow said it intercepted nearly 900 Ukrainian drones; two deaths and a fuel-depot hit are not on the same footing

Reporting

AP reported on 6 October that the Russian Defence Ministry said air defences intercepted nearly 900 Ukrainian drones overnight over multiple regions, occupied Crimea, and the Sea of Azov.
