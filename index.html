Military Overnight 10–11 Sep

Russia launches 161 drones and one Banderol missile; Kyiv residential and gas-station strikes

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported Russia launched 161 attack drones of various types (including jet-powered and Gerbera decoys) plus one S-8000 Banderol low-cost cruise missile overnight into 11 September. Defences shot down or suppressed 138; strikes recorded at 19 locations, debris at three more. In Kyiv’s Dniprovskyi district a nine-storey residential building caught fire; at least eight injured. Later daytime strikes hit two gas stations in Kyiv during morning rush hour, killing two and injuring at least twelve (including a child). Across Ukraine local authorities reported nine killed and 145 injured over the preceding 24 hours. A prior-day strike on a Pavlohrad shopping centre left five dead and 67 injured. Ukrainian forces claimed a strike on the Saratov oil refinery overnight. General Staff reported cumulative Russian personnel losses of approximately 1,503,880, of which 1,490 in the last 24 hours. Russian MoD claimed 777 Ukrainian drones downed over Russian regions and strikes on Ukrainian logistics and data centres.

Equities check

Ukrainian Air Force, General Staff, SES and regional administrations are primary Ukrainian claim-carriers. TASS/Russian MoD are primary Russian claim-carriers. Independent wires (Reuters, AFP, AP) and Kyiv Independent / Ukrainska Pravda corroborate casualty and strike locations but do not independently verify every platform count. ISW assessment of 10 September noted continued long-range Ukrainian strikes and no major ground advances that day.

Judgment

Russia almost certainly continues a high-tempo drone campaign against Ukrainian urban and energy-adjacent targets to impose civilian cost and degrade logistics. Ukrainian long-range strikes on Russian oil infrastructure remain a reciprocal pressure tool. Ground lines of contact showed no decisive change in the window.

Confidence

Moderate-to-high on strike volume and civilian impact (multiple independent desks); moderate on exact platform counts and Russian loss figures (single-side tallies).
