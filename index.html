Moscow’s intercept claim and the two deaths stand; a later 1,750-drone figure is a different Russian tally

Reporting

The same piece said war blogger Yuri Podolyaka claimed a tank at the Volodarskaya fuel depot was hit, and that officials had not confirmed it. Kyiv Post later reported that the Russian Defence Ministry claimed 1,750 fixed-wing drones and 17 Flamingo missiles destroyed over 24 hours, a figure larger than the “nearly 900” command tally carried on 6 October. Judgment
