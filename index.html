A Saturday package hit Kyiv’s Northern Bridge; the Air Force counted 157 drones, and the morning brief’s “no overnight wave” line is corrected

Reporting

The Ukrainian Air Force, as restated by the Kyiv Independent and RBC-Ukraine, said Russia launched 157 attack and decoy drones overnight into 3 October, including Shahed-type drones of which 45 were jet-powered, plus Gerbera and Parodiya decoys, from Bryansk, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, occupied Donetsk, and Crimea. As of 09:00 Kyiv time, 131 were shot down or suppressed; hits were recorded at 12 locations and debris at five. Kyiv Post, citing Mayor Vitali Klitschko, reported an early-Saturday combined ballistic-missile and drone attack that killed one man and wounded two, with fires in Sviatoshynskyi, Obolonskyi, and Holosiivskyi. BBC, with a correspondent in Kyiv, reported that a strike hit the Pivnichnyi (Northern) Bridge, damaging the road and trolleybus wires and blocking left-to-right traffic; two people were injured in that strike account. CNN restated the mayor and the Air Force totals and reported that Russia’s Defence Ministry said it struck the bridges because they move troops and cargo, and again urged foreigners to leave Kyiv. President Zelenskyy posted at 17:06 CEST that a Shahed struck the Northern Bridge, that residential buildings, rail, and energy were hit overnight, and that a drone struck a vessel in Chornomorsk. He also said Ukrainian long-range strikes reached the Samara region, Iskander crews in Bryansk, and the Black Sea. The Kyiv Independent’s day tally was at least eight civilians killed and 81 injured nationwide, including port damage in Odesa region and a morning Banderol attack whose consequences were still being assessed. ISW’s 2 October assessment, posted early 3 October, still covered the prior 108-drone night and judged a September territorial claim by Putin far above its own map. Kremlin.ru, mid.ru, eng.mil.ru, TASS, and Interfax were reviewed; the Defence Ministry bridge claim was reached through BBC and CNN, not as an independent damage count. Rybar and the milblogger layer echoed a winter campaign and are not used for the lead.
