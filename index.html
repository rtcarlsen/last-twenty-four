Military1–2 Oct

Russia kept a near-continuous drone campaign on Kyiv through 1 October: a Shahed hit a school with more than 100 children in shelter, and evening strikes damaged the Southern Bridge; no new overnight package was confirmed before the 2 October cutoff

Reporting

Reuters, citing Kyiv authorities and Mayor Vitali Klitschko, reported that a Russian drone struck a school in the Solomianskyi district on the morning of 1 October. The school director said more than 100 students were present and in shelter; Reuters verified CCTV of the impact. President Zelenskyy posted that more than 100 children and over 60 teachers were in the shelter and that no one was injured, and later said he had spoken with the principal. The Kyiv Independent, citing the Air Force morning bulletin, reported 107 drones including 63 jet-powered, 87 destroyed or suppressed as of 08:00 local, and strikes at 11 locations; local authorities told the same desk at least six people were killed and 38 injured across Ukraine over the preceding day. Klitschko said two drones hit near the supports of the Southern Bridge around 12:20 local; Reuters added that a later evening strike damaged a support, part of the road surface, and protective structures around the metro crossing. Kyiv Post reported early on 2 October that the school warhead failed to detonate; that detail was not in the Reuters account. Kremlin.ru, mid.ru, and eng.mil.ru were reviewed; the Russian Defence Ministry’s 1 October summary, carried by TASS, claimed strikes on energy sites, airfields, and UAV nodes and did not address the school. Rybar and the milblogger layer echoed a winter long-range campaign; that layer is claims only and is not used for the lead. No Reuters, AFP, or Air Force bulletin of a fresh overnight package into the early hours of 2 October was located before cutoff. ISW’s dated assessment for 1 October was not posted in time to use.

Equities check

The Ukrainian Air Force, the Kyiv city administration, and the presidential account are primary claim-carriers for intercept mixes and civilian harm. The Russian Defence Ministry is the primary claim-carrier for its own target set and was not the source of the school account. Reuters is an independent wire with verified imagery. TASS is a state claim-carrier, not a verifier of the MoD text it reprints. Kyiv Post’s warhead claim is a single desk addition. Absence of an early-2-October bulletin is an open-source gap, not proof the night was quiet.

Judgment

Russia almost certainly continued a high-tempo drone campaign against Kyiv through 1 October, consistent with the prior judgment that the 29–30 September energy package opened a winter pressure campaign rather than a one-night raid. The school impact with occupants in shelter is corroborated by an independent wire and city and presidential accounts. Whether a new package was airborne in the hours before this cutoff is not established.

Confidence

High on the school impact and the absence of injuries there; high on continued drone employment on 1 October including the bridge hits; moderate on the evening structural effect (city claim plus a wire restatement); low on the warhead-failure detail (single desk); low on any 2 October overnight package, which was not reported before cutoff.

Confidence

Moderate on Kurti’s stated intent (one national wire relaying a local quote); high on the 6 October deadline as previously set by the court; low on whether a candidate can clear the chamber.
