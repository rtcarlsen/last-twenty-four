Diplomatic5 Sep evening Kremlin

Putin–Witkoff–Kushner Kremlin session ends without an announced deal; next steps deferred to coming weeks

U.S. Special Envoy Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin on Saturday evening, 5 September. Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said the talks lasted three hours and 10 minutes and were constructive and frank.[1][2] A White House official said the two sides discussed substantive plans for next steps, to be announced in the coming weeks.[3] Putin said he ordered a halt to strikes on Kyiv from midnight 5 September and that Moscow never agreed to Ukraine’s broader three-day ceasefire.[4] Stake: Ushakov speaks for the Kremlin; the White House official speaks for the U.S. administration.

Judgment: public product is process language plus a capitals-only pause, not a ceasefire or a published plan.

Confidence: High on the meeting and duration; moderate on substance because no document was released.
