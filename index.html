Military7 Oct

A combined overnight package hit 18 locations; the Pryluky death toll is still moving

Reporting

Ukraine’s Air Force, carried by Kyiv Post and The New Voice of Ukraine, said the strike began at 18:00 on 6 October and used Zircon/Oniks, Iskander-M/S-400/KN-23, and 48 Kh-101, Iskander-K and Kalibr cruise missiles, plus 130 drones of which 46 were jet-powered. By 09:00 the Air Force said 161 targets were neutralised — five ballistic missiles, 39 cruise missiles, and 117 drones — with six further cruise missiles still being verified, hits at 18 locations, and debris at five. Main axes named were Kyiv, Kharkiv, Poltava and Dnipropetrovsk. Reuters reported the Russian Defence Ministry saying it had conducted a “massive strike” on military-industrial sites, naming Fire Point Flamingo and drone facilities in Kyiv and the Kyiv, Chernihiv, Kirovohrad, Dnipropetrovsk, Poltava and Kharkiv regions, plus Odesa port and ship-repair infrastructure. Reuters later quoted Chernihiv governor Vyacheslav Chaus that the Pryluky residential-building toll had risen to 13, including four children, with 50 injured. President Zelenskyy, on his own account at 14:38 UTC, said 18 were dead in Pryluky, four of them children aged one, one and one, and eight, with seven children among the wounded and 15 rescued. BBC, citing Zelenskyy, put the cross-country toll at 24 and said Moscow had not commented on the civilian deaths. The Kyiv Independent’s later update put the national figure at 22, with 18 in Pryluky. kremlin.ru, eng.mil.ru, TASS, and Interfax were reviewed; the ministry wording used here is the Reuters rendering, not a second Russian-language text opened in this pass. ISW’s latest opened assessment was 30 September, not an in-window map. DeepState, Oryx, Mediazona, and Bellingcat were reviewed; no in-window line move or named-dead update was established.

The Russian Defence Ministry speaks for Moscow and frames the same night as a strike on missile and drone production. Reuters is a wire with an adverse-party check on the launch claim. Kyiv Post and NV restate the Air Force. BBC and the Kyiv Independent restate Ukrainian officials. A milblogger echo layer (Rybar, Two Majors, Fighterbomber, Readovka) was reviewed and did not independently support the toll.

Judgment

A large combined Russian strike overnight into 7 October is likely: the Ukrainian Air Force tally and a Russian ministry admission via Reuters describe the same night from opposite interests. The Pryluky residential hit is likely. The exact death toll is not settled — 13 via the governor on Reuters at midday, 18 via the president in the afternoon, and 22 or 24 as national figures on Ukrainian and BBC desks.
