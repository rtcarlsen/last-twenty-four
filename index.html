Military8 Oct

A new drone night hit Kyiv; the Air Force count and the capital’s toll do not yet match

Reporting

The Kyiv Independent, citing the Air Force, said Russia attacked with 142 Shahed-type drones overnight on 8 October, 82 of them jet-powered, and that defenders shot down or suppressed 126. The same desk, citing the Kyiv city military administration, said four people were killed and 21 injured in the capital, including four children: a UAV on a five-storey residential roof in Solomianskyi and debris fire in a 12-storey building in Desnianskyi. Governor Tymur Tkachenko said three people were injured in Kyiv Oblast, in Bucha and Brovary districts. Kyiv Post, citing Mayor Vitali Klitschko, reported three killed and 14 injured in Thursday drone attacks, including a UAV on a four-storey store in Solomianskyi and a later residential hit in Desnianskyi where rescuers found two bodies. A Kyiv Independent live line said a glide-bomb strike on a Kramatorsk bus stop killed at least 33; that figure was not restated in the opened wire copy. The same desk’s main 8 October item put the past-day national toll at least 10 dead and 45 injured. kremlin.ru, eng.mil.ru, TASS and Interfax were reviewed; no Russian ministry text on the 7–8 October night was opened. president.gov.ua and the General Staff account were reviewed; the Air Force wording used here is the Kyiv Independent rendering. Judgment

A Russian drone attack on Kyiv overnight into 8 October is likely. The Kramatorsk figure of at least 33 is single-source, unconfirmed on the desks opened.
