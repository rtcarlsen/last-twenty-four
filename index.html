Military17–18 Sep

Russian overnight package of 93 UAVs (Shahed/Gerbera/Parodiya, 46 jet-powered) plus Banderol/Dan-T; Ukrainian Air Force reports 69–72 aerial targets neutralised; hits at 13 locations; one killed in Zhytomyr industrial park; Ukrainian forces strike Orion UAV control station at Saky airfield in occupied Crimea

Reporting

Ukrainian Air Force reported that overnight into 18 September Russian forces launched 93 strike and decoy UAVs of Shahed, Gerbera and Parodiya types (46 of them jet-powered) plus Banderol/Dan-T munitions from Oryol, Millerovo, occupied Donetsk, Hvardiiske in occupied Crimea and the Sea of Azov. As of 08:00 on 18 September air defences had destroyed or suppressed 69–72 aerial targets. Hits were recorded at 13 locations and debris at three. One person was killed and two injured in a strike on an industrial park in Korosten district, Zhytomyr region. Separately, the General Staff stated that on 17–18 September Ukrainian units struck a ground control station for Orion (Inokhodets) UAVs at Saky airfield near Novofedorivka in occupied Crimea, together with launch sites and depots. Independent wires (Reuters, Interfax-Ukraine) and Ukrainian official channels corroborated the Air Force numbers and the Crimea claim. Russian parliamentary voting began on 18 September; President Putin publicly accused Ukraine of interfering in the election and of hindering peace talks.

Independent wires provide second-tier corroboration of volumes and the Zhytomyr casualty; they cannot independently verify every platform mix or the precise damage at Saky. Putin’s election-interference accusation is a primary Kremlin claim without accompanying evidence in the window.

Judgment

Russia almost certainly conducted another mixed UAV package against Ukrainian rear targets on the night of 17–18 September. Ukrainian forces almost certainly conducted a long-range strike against a Russian UAV control node in occupied Crimea in the same window. Open-source reporting showed no decisive change of control on the ground front. The timing of Putin’s interference claim coincides with the opening of multi-day Duma voting.

Confidence

Moderate-to-high on Ukrainian-reported launch and intercept numbers and on the occurrence of the Saky strike; moderate on exact civilian tolls and precise damage assessments pending further geolocation; high that Putin made the public accusation.

Reporting

Separately, U.S. President Trump signed the Lindsey Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act; Zelenskyy issued a first-person post thanking Trump, the Senate and the House and urging full implementation. Independent wires and president.gov.ua confirmed the summit and the bilateral meetings.
